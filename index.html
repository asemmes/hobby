The Story

Love. Laughter. A Little Deception.

Armand, a gay cabaret owner in South Beach, and his beloved drag queen partner Albert are asked by their son Val to pose as a conventional straight couple — at least for one dinner. The catch? Val's fiancée's parents are staunchly conservative politicians with zero tolerance for anything fabulous. What follows is a glorious collision of worlds, identities, and impeccably timed chaos.
